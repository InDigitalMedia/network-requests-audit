- (insight) Synthesis 1 - One idea in three hats: question chips + funnel coverage grid + per-platform scorecard are the same thing, an 'Answers' layer (platform x outcome) sitting above the request list. This is the headline direction: paid media think in platforms and outcomes, not requests.
- (insight) Synthesis 2 - The keystone is a normalised field layer in tag-detector.js (Event, Value, Currency, Order ID, Dedup ID, Consent, Page, Pixel ID) mapped from each platform's param names, like describeEvent(). Build once and it powers row chips, checklist strip, compact table, receipt cards, cross-platform mismatch checks, CSV and Slack export.
- (insight) Synthesis 3 - Biggest silent risk is absence of evidence: a green verdict when the purchase never fired. Expected-events (vertical templates, tracking plan import, Your setup client profile) is one mechanism that unlocks 'what is missing', unexpected-pixel, and event-name typo checks together.
- (insight) Synthesis 4 - Adoption friction is at the input, not the output: HAR import and a bookmarklet 'Open in decoder' via URL fragment remove DevTools and copy-paste entirely - likely the single biggest win for non-specialists, and both keep the zero-network guarantee (fragment never sent; flag the browser-history exposure).
- (insight) Synthesis 5 - Findings become forwardable actions when phrased as business impact + owner + effort (relabel severities, fix-owner chip, spend-at-risk, Send to developer). Per-client memory in localStorage (baseline diff, ignore rules, pixel names, history) turns a one-off decoder into a recurring check.
- (decision) Suggested sequencing: (1) quick wins on the row - value/currency, consent icons, page, owner chip, collapse healthy + supporting requests, conversions filter, absence banner, Meta case + near-miss names; (2) normalised field layer + Answers scorecard/funnel grid; (3) HAR import + bookmarklet hand-off; (4) per-client memory and expected-events.
