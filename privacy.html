Check tracking – Privacy policy

Applies to the "In Digital – Check tracking" Chrome extension, the "Check tracking" bookmarklet and the tag & tracking checker web page. Last updated 25 November 2026.

In short: it collects nothing and sends nothing. Everything happens inside your own browser, on the page you're looking at, and only when you click it.

What it does

When you click the extension's toolbar icon, it reads the list of network requests the current page has already made and shows the marketing and analytics tags among them (for example Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta, TikTok and LinkedIn) in a panel on that page.

If you click Record payloads, it also watches for tracking requests the page makes after that click, so it can show what they contained. That recording exists only in the open tab and disappears when you close it or move to another page.

What data it collects

None. The extension has no server, no analytics, no accounts and no cookies of its own. It doesn't store anything between visits and doesn't send anything anywhere: not to In Digital, not to Google, not to anyone else.

The panel's Copy all for the decoder button puts the list of requests on your clipboard, so you can paste it into the tag checker page. That only happens when you click it. Where you paste it is up to you, and the checker page also analyses it entirely in your browser.

Permissions it asks for, and why

  • activeTab: lets it see the tab you clicked the icon on, only at the moment you click. It can't see your other tabs, your browsing history, or any page you haven't clicked it on.
  • scripting: lets it show the results panel on that page.

It doesn't ask to "read and change all your data on all websites", and it doesn't run on any page unless you click it there.

Sharing and selling data

Because it collects no data, there is nothing to share, sell or transfer. It doesn't use or pass on anything for advertising, credit-worthiness, lending or any purpose beyond showing you the panel.

Changes
